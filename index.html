While Lovable.dev is excellent for building websites and web apps, Zynch.ai is specifically designed for enterprise business applications. We offer 100% self-hosted deployment, enterprise integrations (SAP, Oracle, etc.), built-in IT governance, and security compliance features that Lovable.dev doesn't provide.

Zynch is deployed entirely within your private I.T. environment — whether that's AWS, Azure, GCP, on-premises, or a hybrid setup. Your data never leaves your infrastructure, ensuring full data sovereignty and compliance.

Zynch supports 50+ enterprise integrations including SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, Microsoft 365, databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, Oracle), and any REST API. We also support custom integrations through our integration framework.

Most applications can be built and deployed within hours or days, not months. Our AI-powered platform transforms your requirements into production-ready code. Complex enterprise integrations may take longer depending on the existing systems.

Zynch is ISO 27001 certified. We support enterprise SSO (SAML, OAuth), role-based access control, end-to-end encryption, and comprehensive audit trails.
