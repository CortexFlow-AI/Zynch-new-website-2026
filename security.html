Security & Compliance

Enterprise-grade security built into our DNA. Your data stays where it belongs - with you.

ISO 27001 Certified

Zynch.ai Ltd. is proud to hold ISO 27001 certification, demonstrating our commitment to the highest standards of information security management. This certification validates that our organization has implemented rigorous security controls and practices to protect your data.

🔒

Self-Hosted by Design

Zynch.ai's solution is self-hosted at the customer's site. Our platform is deployed entirely within your private environment - whether that's your on-premises data center, private cloud, or VPC. Your data never leaves your infrastructure, ensuring complete data sovereignty and compliance with regional regulations.

🚫

Zero Data Access Policy

Zynch.ai does not access, hold, or process any customer data in normal circumstances. Our architecture is designed so that all data processing occurs within your environment. We have no visibility into your business data, customer information, or operational processes.

🔑

Monitored Support Only

Data access occurs only when explicitly requested by the customer through a monitored support session. In such cases, you maintain full control and visibility - every action is logged, every access is authorized by your team, and sessions can be terminated at any time.
